# Code of conduct

ProxyGridVPN follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the
work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted in issues,
pull requests or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **contact@proxygrid.net**. Reports are handled confidentially
by the maintainers listed in [CODE_SIGNING_POLICY.md](CODE_SIGNING_POLICY.md#team-roles), who
may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
